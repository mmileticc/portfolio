About me

Who I am, what I've studied and what I work with.

I'm Milinko, a software engineer from Belgrade. I finished my bachelor's in software engineering at the School of Electrical Engineering (ETF) and I'm now doing my master's there.

I enjoy projects that touch every layer. Movienee, my biggest one, has scrapers for seven cinema chains, a Kotlin/Ktor backend, a native Android app and an Angular web app, and I built all of it. I care about the details that make software feel solid: offline support, sensible error messages, accessibility and fast loading.

Outside of code I play acoustic and classical guitar, and I'm learning the harmonica, which is how GotH and my guitar tuner came to be.
